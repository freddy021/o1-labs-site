Straight answers

Will my clips fill the whole phone screen?

Yes. Every clip is 1080x1920, which is true 9:16 vertical, the same shape as anything you scroll past on TikTok. If your original is a normal landscape video, I reframe it rather than shrinking it into a letterbox. Captions are burned into the picture, so they show whether or not the viewer has sound on, which matters because most people don't.

Can you guarantee views?

No, and I'd be wary of anyone who does. What I guarantee is that strong clips go out consistently instead of not at all, and that I'll tell you honestly what the numbers say each month. The algorithm belongs to nobody. Consistency is the part that's actually within our control, so that's the part I sell.

Why not just use an AI clipping tool for $20 a month?

You can, and for some people that's the right answer. Those tools pick clips by detecting changes in volume and pacing. I read the transcript and ask which single idea would make your actual buyer stop scrolling, then I write the copy in your voice and tell you what to post first. If you have time each week to run a tool and fix its output, save your money. Most people I talk to tried exactly that, and the folder is still empty.

What if I don't like the clips?

Tell me which ones and I'll recut them, no charge and no awkwardness. And if the Pilot isn't useful, don't buy anything else. That's the point of making the first one a single small decision rather than a subscription.

My footage is just a Zoom recording. Is that a problem?

Not at all, that's most of what I work with. Audio quality matters far more than video quality here, because the captions carry the message. If your footage genuinely won't produce good clips, I'll tell you before you pay rather than after.

Who owns the clips?

You do, completely. It's your footage and your ideas. I don't post them anywhere, I don't use them in my own marketing without asking you first, and I keep no rights.

Do I have to get on a call?

No, and that's deliberate. Everything a call would tell you is on this page. Growth and Authority include a strategy session because at that level it's genuinely useful, but nothing is gated behind a sales call. If you'd rather talk anyway, reply to any email and we'll talk.

What's a founding rate?

I'm taking my first ten clients. You'd be betting on someone new, and the discount is what I think that bet is worth. Your rate is locked for 12 months, so you keep it even if prices go up. After ten clients it goes to standard pricing.
